# Peaceful World — Projects

This page is a public map of Peaceful World projects. Some implementation repositories are private while work is still in active development. Public status does not imply open-source licensing unless a repository explicitly says so.

## 5 Practice

A five-minute secular practice designed to help people train more peaceful responses.

**Current focus:** product stability, accessibility, localization, analytics and mobile distribution.

**Ways to help:** testing, accessibility review, localization review, UX feedback and documentation.

**Public tracking:** [stabilize and distribute 5 Practice](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/10)

## 42 Lessons

A structured peace-literacy learning path built around research, reflection and practical application.

**Current focus:** editorial architecture and publication workflow.

**Ways to help:** research review, editing, translation, educational design and source verification.

**Public tracking:** [prepare and publish 42 Lessons](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/11)

## Atlas of Peace

An evolving effort to organize peace-related knowledge, institutions, resources and evidence.

**Current focus:** scope, information architecture and data model.

**Ways to help:** research, data design, taxonomy, visualization and prototype development.

**Public tracking:** [define and prototype Atlas of Peace](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/12)

## True Cost of War

A public-interest data and communication project focused on making the costs of war easier to examine without reducing human lives to abstractions.

**Current focus:** evidence structure, transparency and reusable presentation patterns.

**Ways to help:** source verification, data review, visualization, documentation and accessibility.

**Public tracking:** [improve evidence and visualization](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/14)

## Peaceful World website

The public home of Peaceful World and an increasingly modular set of GitHub-backed pages and interfaces.

**Current focus:** stabilizing the new Home and GitHub-backed Mission, Projects, Impact and History layers; bilingual routing; automated production QA; maintainability, accessibility, performance and public transparency.

**Ways to help:** frontend review, accessibility, QA, performance and documentation.

**Public tracking:** [strengthen Peaceful World web infrastructure](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/13)

## Research and publishing

Long-form work connecting peacebuilding, nonviolence, psychology, philosophy, education and systems thinking.

**Current focus:** rigorous sourcing, clear epistemics and multilingual publication.

**Ways to help:** literature review, fact-checking, editing, translation and subject-matter review.

**Public tracking:** [expand peace-literacy research and publishing](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/16)

## Public GitHub and open collaboration

A public working layer showing current initiatives, contribution opportunities and progress without exposing private release infrastructure or unfinished implementation work.

**Current focus:** public Issues, contribution paths, volunteer sprints and Build Board automation.

**Public tracking:** [build Peaceful World's public GitHub layer](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/issues/15)
