# Peaceful World — Public Work Model

Peaceful World uses GitHub as a public working layer, not as a complete mirror of internal operations.

## What each public layer means

- **Public Roadmap (`ROADMAP.md`)** — the strategic direction: what is active now, what is likely next, and what remains exploratory.
- **Public Issues** — concrete initiatives, research tasks, QA work and contribution opportunities that can be discussed in public.
- **Public Build Board** — a visual Kanban layer for public work. It is supplementary; public Issues remain the durable source of truth.
- **Public Ledger** — the record of what actually happened or shipped.
- **peaceful-world.org** — the mission, public explanation and finished-facing experience.

## Why some repositories stay private

Private repositories may contain active release infrastructure, unfinished experiments, credentials configuration, internal QA material or work that is not yet ready for outside contribution.

Public transparency does not require publishing every implementation detail. Our goal is to make priorities, progress, methods, contribution paths and completed work understandable.

## Status language

When the Public Build Board is used, its operational states are:

`Backlog → Todo → In Progress → QA → Done`

- **Backlog** — worth considering, but not committed for near-term work.
- **Todo** — selected for work, not yet started.
- **In Progress** — actively being worked on.
- **QA** — implemented or drafted and being checked before completion.
- **Done** — completed for the scope described by the public item.

Closing an Issue does not necessarily mean a whole product is finished. It means the scope of that Issue is complete.

## Contribution principle

Public tasks should be:

1. understandable without private context;
2. small enough to complete or review;
3. safe to discuss publicly;
4. connected to a real Peaceful World need;
5. explicit about the expected deliverable.
