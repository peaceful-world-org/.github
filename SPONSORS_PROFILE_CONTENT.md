## Profile details → Introduction

## Help make peace a skill anyone can practice

**Peaceful World** is an independent, nonprofit association registered in Estonia. We create practical tools, research, and learning resources that help people understand nonviolence, strengthen human dignity, and develop more peaceful responses in everyday life.

Peace education should be accessible to everyone, not limited by language, geography, or income.

### What we are building

- **[5 Practice](https://5.peaceful-world.org/):** A five-minute secular practice for more peaceful thinking and action.
- **[True Cost of War](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/blob/main/PROJECTS.md):** Public-interest data and visual explanations that make the human consequences of war easier to understand.
- **Peace literacy and research:** Evidence-informed educational resources about nonviolence, de-escalation, and peacebuilding.
- **Public-interest digital infrastructure:** Accessible websites, localization, documentation, and ways for volunteers to contribute.

### What your sponsorship supports

Your sponsorship helps us maintain learning tools, improve accessibility, translate resources, verify research, and sustain the digital infrastructure behind free peace education.

We publish our priorities and contribution opportunities through a [public roadmap](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) and [project hub](https://github.com/peaceful-world-org/.github/blob/main/PROJECTS.md). Some implementation repositories remain private during active development; public visibility does not imply that every repository is open-source licensed.

**Sponsorship supports the mission, not editorial influence or exclusive control over our work.**
