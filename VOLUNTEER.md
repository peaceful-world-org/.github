# Volunteer with Peaceful World

Peaceful World welcomes people who want to contribute practical skills to peace education and public-interest infrastructure.

You do **not** need to be a developer, and you do not need to make a long-term commitment. Small, finished contributions are often more useful than ambitious promises.

## Where help is useful

### Software and web
- frontend implementation;
- accessibility fixes;
- QA and regression testing;
- performance improvements;
- small automation and tooling tasks.

### Research and data
- literature review;
- source verification;
- data cleaning and structuring;
- taxonomy and information architecture;
- visualization review.

### Language and editorial
- translation and localization review;
- proofreading and editing;
- checking whether language is clear across cultures;
- documentation.

### Design and product
- UX review;
- interaction design;
- accessible visual systems;
- user testing;
- product critique.

## How to start

1. Read the [public roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [projects overview](PROJECTS.md).
2. Look for public issues marked `help wanted`, `good first issue` or `volunteer`.
3. If a task interests you, comment before starting substantial work so effort is not duplicated.
4. Keep the first contribution small and reviewable.
5. Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## What a good volunteer task looks like

A useful task should have:
- a clear outcome;
- enough context to begin;
- a realistic scope;
- no need for access to sensitive information;
- a named review path.

We will prefer tasks that can be completed in hours or days rather than open-ended commitments.

## Current contribution areas

We are preparing public tasks around:
- 5 Practice accessibility and localization;
- research and source verification;
- public website QA and performance;
- Atlas of Peace data and information architecture;
- public-interest data visualization;
- documentation and multilingual publishing.

## Security

Never report vulnerabilities, exposed credentials or other sensitive security issues in a public issue. Use our [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## Other ways to help

If contributing time is not practical, you can [support Peaceful World financially](https://peaceful-world.org/help) or share our work with people who may find it useful.
